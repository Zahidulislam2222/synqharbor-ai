# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical problems clearly;
do not use harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or impersonation.
Respect confidentiality and intellectual-property rights.

Keep project channels relevant. Do not publish personal information or credentials.
Security concerns use SECURITY.md. Contact the maintainer through the contact page
to arrange private handling of a sensitive conduct issue.

The maintainer may remove harmful material or restrict disruptive participation.
Enforcement should be proportionate and protect affected people. Participation
creates no employment or commercial support agreement.
